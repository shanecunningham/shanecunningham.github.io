I'm Shane Cunningham, a systems and software engineer. These days I work on production Kubernetes infrastructure: ingress and networking, and managing clusters at scale.

This blog started in 2010 as notes to myself about whatever I was building or breaking at the time: whitebox ESXi servers, OpenStack, pfSense, ZFS and plenty of Linux. Older posts are marked as archived. They are kept as they were written, so some details will be out of date.

You can find my code on [GitHub](https://github.com/shanecunningham).
