Useful commands

**Verify config**\
/usr/local/nagios/bin/nagios -v /usr/local/nagios/etc/nagios.cfg

**Plugin directory**\
/usr/local/nagios/libexec

**Commands directory**\
/usr/local/nagios/etc/objects
